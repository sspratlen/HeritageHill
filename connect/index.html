While You're Here

What to Expect Today

A few things worth knowing during your visit.

1

You've Already Been Welcomed

Our greeters are real people who are genuinely glad you're here — not scripted salespeople. Need directions anywhere in the building? Just ask.

2

Your Kids Are Taken Care Of

Safe, fun, age-appropriate kids ministry is running right now for birth through 5th grade.

3

Come As You Are

No dress code, no performance required. Whatever you're wearing right now is exactly right.

No Pressure, Ever

We won't ask you to stand up, introduce yourself, or put your hand in the air. Just relax, experience, and see if Heritage Hill feels like home.
