Emailing Your Group Tracking Attendance
For Small Group Leaders

Two things worth five minutes to learn.

Everything below lives in one place — the Small Groups tab of your admin dashboard, right on your own group's row. No separate app, nothing to install.

Start every time the same way:

Log in → Sidebar: Small Groups → Find your group's row
1 Workflow One

Emailing Your Group

Send a message to everyone in your group — announcements, this week's plan, a change of location — without collecting anyone's email address yourself.

1

Open the composer

On your group's row, click ✉ Email Group. A window opens with everything pre-filled for you.

2

Check who it's going to

The To field is already filled in from your group's current members — one email per line. Add or remove someone if the list needs adjusting.

FromYou, as your group's leader
ToPre-filled from current members · editable
3

Write it like a normal email

Add a Subject, then write your message in the box below it. Bold, bullet points, and links all work — it's a real editor, not a plain text box.

Email Group
Subject
This week: we're meeting at the Andersons'
Message
Hey everyone! Quick change for Thursday — we're at 412 Maple St this week instead...
Send Email
4

Send it

Click Send Email. Each person gets their own copy, addressed to them — nobody sees the full recipient list.

✓ That's the whole workflow. No separate email account, no group text chain to manage.
2 Workflow Two

Tracking Attendance

A running log of how many people showed up each week — nobody's names, just a headcount. Takes about ten seconds after your group wraps up.

1

Open your attendance log

On your group's row, click Attendance. A window opens titled with your group's name, showing every week you've logged so far.

2

Log this week

Fill in three things, then click Add:

DateThe date your group met
HeadcountHow many people were there
NotesOptional — e.g. "Holiday week"

3

It's saved — and stays editable

Your entry drops straight into the list below the form, newest on top. Made a typo on the headcount? Click any row to fix it later — nothing is locked in once you hit Add.

✓ That's it — one entry per week your group meets, and your church leadership can see the trend over the whole semester.

A few things people ask

I don't see my group in the list.

You'll only see the group(s) you're listed as the leader for. If nothing shows up, have a staff member confirm you're set as the leader on your group's record.

Can I email just one person instead of the whole group?

Yes — before sending, delete every line in the To field except the one person you want to reach.

What if we didn't meet one week?

Log a headcount of 0 with a note like "Skipped — holiday." That keeps your attendance history accurate instead of leaving a gap.

Does emailing the group cost anything or use my personal email?

No — it sends from the church's system, not your personal inbox, and there's no limit on how often you use it.
