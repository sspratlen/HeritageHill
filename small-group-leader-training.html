Small Group Leader Training

Two things worth five minutes to learn: emailing your group, and logging weekly attendance.

Before We Start

Everything lives in one place.

No separate app, nothing to install. Start the same way every time:

Log in → Sidebar: Small Groups → Find your group's row
Part One

Emailing Your Group

Announcements, this week's plan, a change of location — sent to everyone in your group, without collecting a single email address yourself.

Emailing Your Group

Four steps, start to finish.

1

Open the composer

On your group's row, click ✉ Email Group.

2

Check who it's going to

To is pre-filled from your current members — add or remove anyone.

3

Write it like an email

Subject, then your message — bold, bullets, and links all work.

4

Send it

Click Send Email. Each person gets their own copy.

Part Two

Tracking Attendance

A running headcount for every week your group meets — no names, just numbers, and it takes about ten seconds.

Tracking Attendance

Three fields, one click.

On your group's row, click Attendance, then fill in:

DateThe date your group met
HeadcountHow many people were there
NotesOptional — e.g. "Holiday week"

Click Add — it's saved, and stays editable if you need to fix it later.

A Few Things People Ask

Quick answers.

I don't see my group.

You only see groups you're listed as the leader for — ask staff to confirm your name is on the record.

Just one person, not the whole group?

Delete every line in To except that one person before sending.

We didn't meet one week.

Log a headcount of 0 with a note — that keeps the history accurate instead of leaving a gap.

Does this use my personal email?

No — it sends from the church's system, with no limit on how often you use it.

You're Ready

Go lead your group.

Small Groups tab → your group's row → Email Group or Attendance.

Questions? Ask your Small Groups staff contact.
